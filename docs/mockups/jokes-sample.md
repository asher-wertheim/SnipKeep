## Rules for the full bank

- Original jokes only, family-friendly, English.
- No two jokes with the same punchline or setup. A build-time check rejects near duplicates.
- Each joke has an optional AI comment. Not every joke needs one.
- No jokes about real people, brands in a bad light, or sensitive topics.
